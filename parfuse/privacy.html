Privacy Policy for Parfuse

Effective date: October 2026

Parfuse is made by Gorvaniq ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: we do not collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. Parfuse has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, or share your name, email address, location, device identifiers, or anything else about you.

What stays on your device

The app saves your game progress, streak, results, and settings on your own device so the game can remember them. This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules notifications on your phone. They are created and shown by your phone; nothing is sent to us.

Feedback

If you tap "Send feedback", the app opens your own email app with a message to us. It includes the app version, your phone's browser version string, whether you have the unlock, and the app's recent error messages, so we can fix bugs. You can read, edit or delete any of it, and nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. We use feedback emails only to answer you and fix problems.

Backups

Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account (Android's backup, if you have it turned on), so your progress comes back on a new phone. We cannot see these backups.

Purchases

If you buy the optional unlock, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. Google may share purchase confirmation with us so the app can unlock your features. Google's handling of your information is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Sharing

When you tap the share button, the app creates a short text of your result. It is shared only if you choose to paste or send it somewhere. It contains no personal information.

Children

Parfuse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
